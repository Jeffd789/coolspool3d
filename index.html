CoolSpool3D Publishing

Our local publishing application prepares and schedules the owner's 3D-print videos. With the owner's authorization, it uploads videos and thumbnails to YouTube and reads channel identity and video status to confirm delivery. It also publishes to the owner's Facebook Page and connects other social destinations through Buffer.

This is an owner-operated tool, not a public sign-up service. Authorization happens on the platform's own consent screen; this website does not collect social-media passwords.
