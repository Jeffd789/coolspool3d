Privacy policy

CoolSpool3D Publishing · Updated October 15, 2026

CoolSpool3D Publishing is a local tool operated by the owner of the CoolSpool3D channels. It is not offered as an account-management service to the public. Contact: coolspool3d@gmail.com.

What the application accesses

When the owner connects YouTube, the application requests permission to upload videos and read YouTube account information. It uses channel IDs and names to verify the intended destination, uploads the owner's video files, titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and reads video IDs and processing/publication status to confirm uploads. It receives OAuth access and refresh tokens. It does not request Gmail, contacts, Google Drive, payment information or social-media passwords.

The Facebook connection uses a Page access token to publish the owner's videos, read Page and publication information, and manage video covers. Its permission bundle includes Page engagement and user-content access. Instagram, TikTok and X publishing uses the owner's Buffer connection. Platform tokens and permissions remain subject to their providers' controls.

Purpose, storage and sharing

Data is used to publish the owner's approved content, schedule delivery, detect errors and prevent duplicate posts. Credentials are encrypted with Windows DPAPI on the owner's PC. Local publishing records retain channel and video identifiers, upload progress and delivery status. The operator can inspect these records for support and recovery.

Publishing sends selected video files and metadata to their intended social platform, and where necessary to Buffer and the configured media host so the platform can retrieve them. Published content is visible according to its platform privacy setting. Private Google account data is not sold, used for advertising, transferred to data brokers or used to train generalized AI models. Google user data is used only for the publishing and verification features described here.

CoolSpool3D Publishing's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Credentials are retained while the owner keeps the integration connected. Local delivery records are retained for posting history, recovery and duplicate suppression. The owner can remove local credentials and records, or request deletion through the contact address above. Removing local data does not automatically delete videos already published to social platforms.

YouTube access can be revoked in Google Account connections. Facebook, Buffer and other platform access can be revoked in the respective account settings. Revocation stops future authorized API access; existing local records must be removed separately. Do not send passwords or tokens by email.

This website

This static information site has no login, tracking scripts or analytics cookies installed by CoolSpool3D. GitHub Pages provides hosting and may process request information under GitHub's privacy statement. Following social links takes you to services governed by their own policies.

Changes

This page will be updated if the application's data practices change. Any materially different access or use requires fresh owner authorization.
